Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Waterloo

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed

What a Professional Brings to Storm Damage Repair

The repairs a storm leaves behind are tangled together, and sorting them out correctly calls for an experienced restoration crew. Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
