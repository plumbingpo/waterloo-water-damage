Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Across Waterloo, Waterloo Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

How fast and how completely water is extracted decides how much of your property is damaged and what restoration will cost. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. We go after those hidden deposits with specialty tools and methods, and that takes out far more water than extracting the surface only. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Add our number, (954) 206-3841, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
